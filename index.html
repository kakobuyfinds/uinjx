Three buying guides

What to check before ordering

Keep one row per seller and variant. Record the item ID, selected size, when you checked the listing, and any measurement or photo you still need.

Verify FSBuy product links

Open the original marketplace listing and compare its item ID, seller name, model and color with the spreadsheet row. A Weidian product link or Taobao listing can stay live after a seller changes its variants. On a 1688 listing, check whether the price assumes a minimum order quantity.

Save the original URL before handing it to your chosen agent. If a link redirects to a different product, check the replacement as a new listing.

Use an FSBuy QC checklist

QC means quality control. Ask for warehouse photos of the actual item and give the warehouse one precise instruction per photo. For shoes, ask for a ruler across the full insole length. For tops, request a flat chest-width measurement from armpit to armpit.

Compare the selected variant with the size label and close-ups of seams, cuffs or hardware. If the photos leave a question open, check the seller’s return window while you can still request another angle.

Estimate FSBuy shipping costs

Put the item price, domestic shipping, any agent or payment fees and international delivery in the same currency. Keep the exchange rate and quote date with your notes so you can compare two orders on equal terms.

Some routes bill by volumetric weight. Enter the packed carton dimensions and actual parcel weight in the carrier or agent calculator. Check destination taxes and handling charges separately when estimating the total delivered cost.

Fit and finish

Measurements worth asking for

Measure something you already wear comfortably, using the same method and units as the seller’s size chart.

Shoes
Compare sneaker insole length, toe-box width and heel shape with a pair you own. Ask for an outsole photo if tread condition matters.
Hoodies, sweaters and tees
Compare flat chest width, shoulder seam placement and body length. Ask for the fabric weight if you need a lighter layer.
Jackets
Compare sleeve length over the layers you expect to wear. Ask for photos of the zipper teeth, lining and cuff closure.
Pants, shorts and sets
Check flat waistband width, front rise and inseam. For a matching set, confirm whether you can choose top and bottom sizes separately.
Headwear, jerseys and underwear
Compare cap crown depth and strap range, jersey chest width, or waistband stretch against the relevant size chart.
Bags and jewelry
Check bag strap drop and attachment points. For bracelets, compare inner circumference and ask for a close-up of the clasp.

Quick answers

FSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an FSBuy spreadsheet?

An FSBuy spreadsheet is a category-organized list of product leads you can use to compare listings before ordering through a shopping agent. Start by checking the live seller page for the exact model, size and color.

Where do the category links take me?

They open category pages on HipobuyFind. This independent guide has no official affiliation with FSBuy or the pictured brands.

Which QC photos should I request for sneakers?

Ask for both side profiles, the outsole, the size label and a ruler photo of the insole. Compare the measurement with a pair that fits you and check that the photos show your selected color and size.

What if a Weidian, Taobao or 1688 link stops working?

Look for the original item ID in your notes. If you find another listing, verify the seller, variant and order quantity before adding it to your shortlist.

Can I estimate shipping before buying?

Yes. Use the destination, estimated packed weight and carton dimensions for a preliminary quote. Update the estimate once the parcel is packed and check any taxes or handling charges that apply.

Do the brand photos show items available through these links?

The photos illustrate details to check. Check each live seller listing for availability and ask for separate evidence of authenticity if it matters to your purchase.
